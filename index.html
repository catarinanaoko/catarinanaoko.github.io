catarinanaoko.com

In a lucky draw, Catarina Naoko is interested in the moment of breaking a fortune cookie - the anticipation and the message, the intimacy of two fingers and a prophecy.

Taking vulnerability at its core, her works combines narrative with graphic, editorial, and digital design.

Much like a fortune cookie - it is a nod to the magic of everyday life, showing how even a simple phrase can connect people.
